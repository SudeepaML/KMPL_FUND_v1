## Brand & Style

This design system targets rural and semi-urban community sports organizers, team captains, and village tournament stakeholders managing finances, player registrations, and tournament rules on mobile Android devices. The aesthetic merges the intense energy of night-match floodlights with the precision of financial accounting. 

The visual style is **High-Contrast Dark Surface**, rooted in pure `#000000` canvas depths to maximize battery conservation under bright outdoor sunlight while delivering electric contrast indoors and at evening matches. Crisp, vivid accent lines slice across tiered dark grey surfaces, evoking cricket turf markings, scoreboards, and stadium telemetry. 

The interface must evoke:
- **Absolute Trust & Transparency:** Every rupee collected or spent is rendered with high legibility and explicit validation states.
- **Grassroots Sporting Pride:** Vibrant emerald green reflects the manicured pitch, balanced with energetic pinks and rulebook ambers.
- **Zero-Friction Ergonomics:** Optimized for one-handed outdoor usage on variable Android screen sizes, high-glare conditions, and multilingual contexts (English and Kannada script compatibility).

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep AMOLED black mobile displays, ensuring exceptional clarity in high-ambient-light environments.

### Core Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#000000`):** The absolute background. Zero illumination, grounding all layers.
- **Surface Tier 1 (`#121212`):** Primary cards, list backdrops, and bottom sheets.
- **Surface Tier 2 (`#1E1E1E`):** Elevated modals, floating containers, and interactive list tiles.
- **Surface Tier 3 (`#2A2A2A`):** Toggled active states, dividers, and input field backdrops.

### Accent Spectrum & Domain Meanings
- **Pitch Green (`#00E676` base, `#10B981` pressed/deep):** The system primary. Reserved exclusively for tournament branding, financial approvals, active language selector pills, and Admin action CTAs.
- **Tournament Gold / Rules Yellow (`#FACC15` primary, `#EAB308` border/shade):** Reserved for tournament rule badges, caution notices, match dispute entries, and pending balance tags.
- **Player Portal Pink (`#EC4899` core, `#F43F5E` gradient edge, `#FFFFFF` high-contrast pairing):** Represents dynamic player interaction, roster cards, registration drives, and animated micro-interactions.
- **Developer & Sandbox Pink (`#F472B6`):** Dedicated identifier for development toggles, sandbox indicators, and mock transaction states.
- **Text & Foregrounds:**
  - Primary Content: `#FFFFFF` (100% opacity for key amounts, headings, Kannada text).
  - Secondary Content: `#A1A1AA` (Medium grey for metadata, timestamps, balance labels).
  - Outlines & Borders: `#27272A` (Subtle boundary definition between stacked dark surfaces).

## Typography

Typography is set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its tall x-height, open apertures, and crisp rendering on dense Android DPI matrixes. Its modern geometric curves cleanly pair alongside native Kannada system typefaces (such as Noto Sans Kannada) without visual jarring or vertical clipping.

### Font System & Script Adaptability
- **Kannada Alignment:** All line heights maintain a minimum 1.35x ratio to accommodate Kannada conjuncts (vattakshara) and vowel diacritics without clipping top or bottom extremities.
- **Financial Figures:** Currency tallies and balance displays use bold or extrabold tabular weights to align rupee denominations vertically across cards and ledger rows.
- **Display Scaling:** For screen widths below 360dp, `display-lg` scales down to 30px (`lineHeight: 38px`), ensuring full rupee totals fit onto a single line without awkward wrapping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses a **fluid 4-column grid** on Android smartphones (up to 599dp), expanding to an **8-column layout** on foldables and tablets (600dp+). 

### Mobile-First Spacing Rhythm
- **Outer Canvas Margins:** 16px (`1rem`) on standard mobile devices; 24px (`1.5rem`) on tablets.
- **Card & Surface Gutters:** Fixed 16px (`1rem`) horizontal gap between columns to allow comfortable thumb scanning.
- **Ergonomic Safe Zones:** Bottom action bars and critical Admin CTAs maintain a minimum 16px clearance above the Android system navigation bar (gesture bar or 3-button nav).
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Standard stack intervals between distinct ledger blocks utilize `space-md` (16px), while tight sub-elements (e.g., player tags under names) use `space-xs` (4px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is constructed via **Tonal Surface Stacking** coupled with **Thin High-Contrast Ghost Borders**, avoiding heavy diffuse shadows that muddy AMOLED displays.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Floor 0 (Canvas):** `#000000` — viewport root.
2. **Floor 1 (Containers):** `#121212` with a 1px solid border of `#27272A`. Hosts tournament summaries, player lists, and balance ledgers.
3. **Floor 2 (Floating Elements & Dialogs):** `#1E1E1E` with a 1px border of `#3F3F46`. Used for bottom sheets, quick entry forms, and language switchers.
4. **Floor 3 (Active Overlays & Alerts):** `#2A2A2A` bordered with appropriate semantic accent highlights (e.g., `#00E676` for financial confirmations, `#FACC15` for pending disputes).

### Tactical Glow Accents
Subtle localized directional glow is permitted only on primary action nodes:
- Admin primary submission buttons utilize a 0px 4px 16px blur with `#00E676` at 25% opacity.
- Live Player Portal elements use a dual-tone subtle rim light (`#EC4899` at 20% opacity) when active.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy follows **Level 2 (Rounded)**, balancing modern Android Material 3 tactile familiarity with structured financial rigor.

- **Base Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to list tiles, input text boxes, and table rows.
- **Container Radius (16px / `1rem`):** Applied to transaction summary cards, rule sheets, and dialog surfaces.
- **Hero & Interactive Capsule (24px / `1.5rem` to Pill):** Filter tags, bilingual English/Kannada toggles, status pills, and prominent FAB (Floating Action Button) containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Touch Ergonomics:** All touch targets maintain a strict minimum height of 48px, padded to 52px for primary thumb targets.
- **Admin CTA (Primary):** Solid background in `#00E676`, text in `#000000` (800 weight), 12px horizontal padding, 16px corner radius. Glow of `#00E67633` on press.
- **Player Portal CTA:** `#EC4899` background with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-animated gradient transition shifting between `#EC4899` and `#F43F5E` when idle/ready.
- **Secondary / Action Outlines:** Background `#121212`, 1.5px border `#27272A`, text `#FFFFFF`. On active touch, border transitions to `#00E676`.

### Language Selector (EN / KN)
- Pill-shaped segmental toggle container (`#1E1E1E`, 40px height).
- Selected segment fills with `#00E676` at 15% opacity with a 1.5px `#00E676` border, text set in white bold. Non-selected segment remains muted `#A1A1AA`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Rules / Caution Badge:** `#FACC15` fill at 12% opacity, 1px border `#FACC15`, text `#FACC15`, `label-sm` font, pill radius (9999px), 6px vertical by 10px horizontal padding.
- **Verified / Settled Badge:** `#00E676` fill at 12% opacity, 1px border `#00E676`, text `#00E676`.
- **Developer Mode Pill:** `#F472B6` dotted 1px border, `#1E1E1E` background, `#F472B6` text.

### Cards & Ledger Tiles
- **Tournament Overview Card:** Background `#121212`, 16px corner radius, 1px `#27272A` border. Top border features a 3px accent line in Pitch Green (`#00E676`).
- **Ledger Item Tile:** Minimum height 56px, background `#121212`, horizontal padding 16px. Left side displays transaction title and bilingual subtitle; right side displays bold tabular amount (`+₹` in `#00E676`, `-₹` in `#EF4444`).

### Input Fields
- Filled container style with `#1E1E1E` base, 8px radius, bottom stroke 2px `#27272A`.
- Focus state activates bottom stroke and label tint in `#00E676`.
- Helper text explicitly supports dual-line English and Kannada guidance without overlapping vertical flow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Minimum 48px touch bounding box enclosing a 20px visible element.
- Checked state: `#00E676` background with sharp `#000000` checkmark.
- Unchecked state: `#1E1E1E` background with `#3F3F46` border (2px).
